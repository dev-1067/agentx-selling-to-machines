How it works

Deciding is code. Talking is checked.

The conversation can't move the price. Four small parts, each with one job, between the buyer's message and SAUDA's reply.

01

Strategist

Picks a basket price each round from numbers only: the buyer's bids, the policy floor and the round. It fits the buyer's concession curve and predicts its limit.

02

Policy guard

Clamps every offer between the floor and the list price and within stock. Refuses bids with slipped-in out-of-stock lines or ₹1 add-ons.

03

Truth gate

Checks every sentence against the official truthfulness rules and a ledger of the numbers actually in play. A sentence that fails is replaced before it is sent.

04

Tactic classifier

A trained model names the buyer's trick: fake quote, fake authority, prompt injection and more. It picks calm wording, never a discount.

FAQ

Good questions.

Can a buyer talk SAUDA into a lower price?

No. Prices are computed from the basket and the buyer's priced bids only. Words, threats and fake quotes are labelled for the audit log and answered calmly, but they never enter the price calculation.

What stops it from saying something untrue?

Every outgoing sentence passes a truth gate that re-implements the official truthfulness checks and a ledger of numbers actually in play. Failing sentences are replaced with a plain, true one.

Does it ever deadlock?

It is built to close or walk away before the last round. On the official run it deadlocked once in 210 negotiations, against 21 for the starter baseline.

Does it need any outside service?

No. SAUDA runs entirely on its own, in under 2 ms per reply, with no paid API. An optional adapter can also send each claim to an external verifier such as Rezolve TraceWare; it is off by default and fails closed.

Is my account data safe?

Passwords are never stored, only salted PBKDF2 hashes. Sessions are random tokens in an HttpOnly cookie. Everything stays in a local SQLite file.
